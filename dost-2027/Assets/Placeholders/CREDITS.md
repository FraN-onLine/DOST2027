# Placeholder art: credits

Free stand-ins for the art the team has not drawn yet. Everything here is **CC0 1.0
(public domain)** by [Kenney](https://kenney.nl) ([Kenney Vleugels](https://kenney.nl)), so
there is no attribution duty and no licence trouble for the competition. We credit him
anyway. The two packs were downloaded from kenney.nl and each picture was converted to
white on transparent (so the game can tint it) and resized to the size in the table;
nothing else was changed.

Packs:

- **Board Game Icons** 1.1: <https://kenney.nl/assets/board-game-icons> (CC0)
- **Particle Pack** 1.1: <https://kenney.nl/assets/particle-pack> (CC0)
